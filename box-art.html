📦 This is the picture on the box — the plan for what it'll look like and how it'll work. Nothing gets built until you sign off on the 3 “done” conditions below.
ConfidenceQuest is a vibrant arcade RPG in your browser: name what's scaring you (interview, presentation, hard conversation, date), customize your Companion creature, then fight through FIVE genuinely fun mini-games — a rhythm platformer, a thought-zapping wave shooter, a strength-collecting lab, a STAR Forge that trains you to nail behavioral 'tell me about a time…' interview answers (Situation → Task → Action → Result), and a climactic boss battle against a Doubt Boss themed to your exact event. Your Companion visibly grows bolder, gains badges, and unlocks new body types, colors, and accessories as you level up. No login, no server — runs entirely in your browser and remembers your progress.

How it works
1
Player arrives at the live URL, types the name of what scares them ('job interview at Google'), and picks a Companion creature — body shape, color, and expression style from a small palette.
→
2
The game maps the entry to one of four Boss archetypes (Interrogator / Critic / Gatekeeper / Unknown) and personalizes all mini-game dialogue and the Boss skin to that archetype plus the player's exact typed event name.
→
3
Player travels the World Map, choosing nodes in any order: Breathe Runner (rhythm platformer — jump to breath cadence), Thought Buster (wave-shooter — zap anxious-thought gremlins and watch reframes explode in their place), Power-Up Lab (collect Courage Crystals by naming real strengths and past wins), and STAR Forge (a behavioral-interview trainer — assemble a real 'tell me about a time…' story into the correct Situation → Task → Action → Result order to forge a Courage weapon, earning bonus points for a complete, well-structured answer).
→
4
Each completed node fills the Confidence Stat panel, and the Companion visibly levels up — gains glow, color intensity, a badge, and unlocks new customization options (body types, colors, accessories) — with a particle burst and level-up sound cue.
→
5
When the four training nodes are cleared, the BOSS BATTLE unlocks: the Doubt Boss themed to the player's archetype appears in full illustrated glory. The player builds a Courage Meter by choosing event-specific courage-responses in a Pokémon-style turn sequence until the meter is full and the Boss is defeated.
→
6
On victory, the Companion reaches its final evolved form, the dashboard shows 100% Confidence Stat, and the player sees a personalized readiness summary — all saved to localStorage so they can return the morning of the real event and see their wins.

The one hard part we pre-mapped
The wall: Delivering four genuinely distinct arcade mini-games (real-time rhythm platformer, wave-shooter, collection mechanic, turn-based boss battle) that all share a single live Companion/stat state in a zero-dependency static browser app — without a game engine or server — while keeping animation feeling juicy (particles, level-up bursts, responsive controls) across desktop and mobile. Candidate: build each mini-game as a self-contained Canvas/DOM module reading from and writing to one shared plain-JS GameState singleton serialized to localStorage; use requestAnimationFrame loops per module and a lightweight vanilla Canvas 2D particle system; Companion visual state driven by CSS class set from GameState.level so all modules always agree. Fallback: simplify Breathe Runner and Thought Buster to DOM-animated interactions instead of Canvas — less arcade-juicy but still interactive — keeping Boss Battle as the only full Canvas module and leaving the shared-state architecture identical.
